# Data sources and licenses

`data/hanzi.json.gz` is built from these dictionaries by `build_data.py`. Thanks to everyone who maintains them.

| Source | Used for | License |
|---|---|---|
| [CC-CEDICT](https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cedict) | Mandarin words, pinyin, English meanings | [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) |
| [Unihan database](https://www.unicode.org/charts/unihan.html) (Unicode, Inc.) | Second source for every character reading; character variants | [Unicode License v3](https://www.unicode.org/license.txt) |
| [rime-cantonese](https://github.com/rime/rime-cantonese) | Cantonese readings (Jyutping) | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| [KANJIDIC2](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/KANJIDIC_Project) | Japanese character readings and meanings | [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html), Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group |
| [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project) | Japanese words | [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html), Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group |
